# Electric-Vehicle-Adoption-Analysis

**Project Overview**
---

This project explores electric vehicle adoption patterns across the United States using publicly available vehicle registration data obtained from Data.gov. Using Power BI, I developed an interactive dashboard to examine adoption trends, geographic distribution, vehicle characteristics, manufacturer market share, and electric range performance to better understand the evolving electric vehicle landscape.

**Power BI Dashboard Screenshot**
---

The dashboard includes KPIs, geographic visualizations, manufacturer comparisons, vehicle model distributions, and electric vehicle type breakdowns. The dashboard highlights approximately 300,000 electric vehicles with filters enabling deeper exploration of adoption trends.

**Business Question**
---

How are electric vehicles distributed across geographic regions, manufacturers, vehicle types, and model years, and what insights can be derived regarding EV adoption trends?

**Project Objectives**
---

- Explore national electric vehicle adoption patterns using publicly available vehicle registration data.
- Examine geographic differences in EV distribution.
- Identify dominant manufacturers and vehicle models within the EV market.
- Compare battery electric vehicles (BEVs) and plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs).
- Assess electric range differences across manufacturers.
- Develop an interactive dashboard that transforms raw data into actionable insights.

**Data Sources**
---

- Publicly available Electric Vehicle Population data obtained from Data.gov
- Dataset fields included:
    - Vehicle manufacturer
    - Vehicle model
    - Model year
    - Electric vehicle type
    - Electric range
    - County and state location
    - Utility provider information
    - Alternative fuel eligibility indicators

**Methodology**
---

This project utilized exploratory data analysis and interactive dashboard development techniques.

- Imported and reviewed public EV registration data.
- Performed basic data validation and field formatting in Excel and Power BI.
- Created calculated summaries and aggregations using Power BI.
- Developed interactive visualizations to analyze vehicle adoption, manufacturer trends, geographic distribution, and electric range characteristics.
- Implemented dashboard filters to allow users to explore data by county, manufacturer, utility provider, and vehicle type.

**Tools Used**
---

- Power BI
- Excel

**Key Insights**
---

- Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) represented approximately 81% of all vehicles in the dataset, while Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) represented approximately 19%.
- Tesla accounted for the largest share of electric vehicles among manufacturers included in the dataset.
- The dataset contained approximately 300,000 registered electric vehicles spanning model years 1999 through 2027.
    - The average EV Model Year was 2022
- Vehicle range varied substantially across manufacturers, highlighting differences in battery technology and vehicle design.
- Geographic visualizations revealed uneven EV distribution, with adoption concentrated in specific regions and counties.
    - Washington State account for 99.7% of the total EV vehicle distribution
- This data was last updated as of October 14, 2026.

**Potential Business Applications**
---

- Support state and local governments in understanding EV adoption trends.
- Inform infrastructure planning for electric vehicle charging networks.
- Identify regions with growing demand for EV-related services and utilities.
- Evaluate market concentration among EV manufacturers.
- Monitor changes in electric vehicle technology and range capabilities over time.

**Analytical Limitations**
---

- Data quality is dependent on the accuracy and completeness of publicly reported registration records.
- Geographic concentrations may be influenced by population size, policy environments, and reporting practices that were not analyzed in this project.
    - For instance, reports show that Washington state has adopted the most ambitious clean cars target in the nation, with a goal of having all new passenger vehicles be electric by 2030.
- The dashboard provides descriptive insights and does not attempt to establish causal relationships between policy, demographics, and vehicle adoption.
- Some vehicle records may contain missing or incomplete information related to range, location, or eligibility classifications.

**Conclusion**
---

By combining geographic analysis, vehicle characteristics, manufacturer performance, and adoption trends, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the current electric vehicle landscape and highlights opportunities for further transportation and energy-related research.
